Who We Are

Welcome to RS Drive Automation

RS Drive Automation is your premier destination for automation spare parts. As a leading provider of automation spare parts, we have built a reputation for delivering superior products and exceptional service across New Zealand and Australia.

Our extensive inventory consists of a comprehensive selection of spare parts from renowned manufacturers, ensuring reliability, compatibility, and performance. With a commitment to excellence and a wide product range, we are your trusted partner in industrial automation.

We support businesses in minimising downtime by delivering industrial automation spare parts quickly and dependably. Our approach is built on clear communication and reliable service at every stage — from enquiry and order processing through to after‑sales support. We prioritise fast responses and provide accurate information on stock availability, lead times, and shipping.

Our core service is rapid dispatch from genuine stock held in New Zealand. Where items are not immediately available, we can source parts on request through our established supplier network, subject to condition, availability, and lead time.

We cater to a diverse customer base including manufacturing plants, assembly lines, warehouses and industrial facilities of all sizes. Whether you need motors, sensors, controllers, actuators or any other automation spare part, our knowledgeable team is ready to find the right solution for your specific needs.

At RS Drive Automation, we understand the critical role that automation plays in improving efficiency across industries. Our mission is to provide high-quality automation spare parts that ensure uninterrupted operations and maximise productivity for our customers.
